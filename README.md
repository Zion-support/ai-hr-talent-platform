# AI HR Talent Platform

> Hire faster, onboard smoother, retain longer.

AI talent platform — sourcing, screening, onboarding and retention analytics in one place. Part of the **Zion AI App Network** — 550+ interlinked AI & IT tools by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com).

**Live app:** https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-hr-talent-platform/

## Features
- AI candidate sourcing and skill-based screening
- Structured interview kits with bias checks
- Automated, personalized onboarding journeys
- Retention risk and engagement analytics
- Skills-gap mapping and internal mobility suggestions
- Integrations: Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, BambooHR, Slack

## Use Cases
- Cut time-to-hire with AI shortlists
- Standardize interviews and reduce bias
- Spot attrition risk before resignations land
